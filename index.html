How Priority Queue Works

A Priority Queue stores patients according to emergency severity instead of simple arrival order. Patients with critical conditions are treated first. It is implemented using a Max Heap data structure.

Insert Patient

O(log n)

New patient is added and bubbles up to correct position

View Next Patient

O(1)

Highest priority patient is always at the root
